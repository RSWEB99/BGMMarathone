About RUNADDA Elite Running Academy

OUR STORY

Building a running culture in Belagavi

RUNADDA Elite Running Academy was founded by the Elite Masters Running Team, Belagavi, with a simple mission: make running accessible, safe, and inspiring for every resident of North Karnataka — from first-time joggers to seasoned marathoners.

Since our early morning training groups began on the roads around Belagavi Fort, we've grown into a full-fledged academy offering structured coaching, community runs, and signature events like the Namma Belagavi Marathon — races that blend fitness, heritage, and environmental awareness.

🎽

Structured Coaching

Certified trainers guide runners of every level through personalised training plans.

🌳

Run To Save Nature

Every event we host carries an environmental cause — tree drives, plastic-free routes, and clean-up runs.

🏛️

Rooted In Heritage

Our races honour Belagavi's history — from Kittur Rani Chennamma to Krantiveer Sangolli Rayanna.

FLAGSHIP EVENT

Namma Belagavi Marathon — Run To Save Nature

Held every November along the panoramic Yarmal Rajhansgarh Road, this is North Karnataka's premier road running event — welcoming runners from across India and beyond.
