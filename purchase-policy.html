Purchase Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Registration Fees

Registration fees are set by individual event organizers and displayed on each event's Tickets tab. A small convenience fee and applicable GST are added at checkout.

2. Non-Refundable, Non-Transferable

Unless explicitly stated otherwise by the organizer, confirmed registrations are non-refundable and non-transferable. Please choose your event category carefully before completing payment.

3. Event Cancellation or Postponement

In the rare event that an organizer cancels or postpones an event, RunPulse will communicate updated terms — including any refund or credit options offered by the organizer — via email and SMS.

4. Payment Methods

We support Credit/Debit Cards, UPI, and Netbanking through our secure sandbox payment gateway. All transactions are encrypted in transit.

5. E-Tickets & Bib Collection

Upon successful payment, an e-ticket is generated instantly and can be viewed or printed from your order confirmation page. Physical bib numbers and race kits are collected at the venue as per the schedule shared by the organizer.

6. Disputes

For any payment discrepancies, please contact support@runpulse.in within 7 days of your transaction along with your Order ID.
