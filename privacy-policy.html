Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an event with RUNADDA Elite Running Academy, we collect information such as your name, email address, mobile number, date of birth, gender, T-shirt size, and emergency contact details. This information is used solely to process your event registration and communicate important race-day updates.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to confirm registrations, issue e-tickets and bib numbers, send event updates, and improve our platform. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Payment Information

All payments on RUNADDA are processed through our secure payment gateway. We do not store your full card number, CVV, or banking credentials on our servers at any point during checkout.

4. Cookies

We use necessary, analytics, and marketing cookies to keep the site secure, remember your preferences, and understand usage patterns. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time from the cookie banner.

5. Data Sharing with Organizers

When you register for an event, relevant registration details (name, contact information, category) are shared with the event organizer to facilitate race-day operations and communication.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting our support team.

7. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, please reach out to us at privacy@runpulse.in.
