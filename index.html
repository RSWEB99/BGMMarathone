ABOUT THE RUN

A race with a purpose

The Namma Belagavi Marathon is organised by the Elite Masters Running Team, Belagavi, as part of RUNADDA Elite Running Academy's mission to build a healthier, greener Karnataka — one stride at a time.

This edition honours the legacy of freedom fighters Kittur Rani Chennamma and Krantiveer Sangolli Rayanna, weaving heritage into every kilometre of the scenic Belagavi-to-Yarmal route.
